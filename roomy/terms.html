What Roomy does

Roomy helps you find and remove duplicate, similar, blurry and very dark photos, large videos and other clutter from your phone gallery, and lets you sort photos by swiping. Its suggestions are automatic and may not always be right, so please check before you delete.

Deleting is your decision

Roomy never deletes anything on its own. Items are removed only after you confirm in Android's system dialog. Depending on your setting, deleted items are either removed permanently or moved to your gallery trash. Deleted photos and videos may not be recoverable, so please keep a backup of anything important. You are responsible for what you choose to delete, and we are not liable for any loss of data.
