ROOMY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Roomy helps you clean up your phone gallery: it finds duplicate, similar, blurry and very dark photos, large videos and screenshots, and lets you review and delete them. This policy explains what Roomy does and does not do with your data.

Your photos stay on your phone

Roomy analyses your photos and videos only on your device. Your photos, videos and file names are never uploaded, copied or sent to any server by us. We do not use any cloud or AI service to look at your media.

Permissions we ask for

To work, Roomy asks Android for access to your photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or the partial "selected photos" access on newer Android versions). This access is used only to list, preview, analyse and, when you confirm, delete items. Every deletion goes through Android's own confirmation dialog, so nothing is deleted without your approval.

Information stored on your phone

Roomy keeps a few things locally in the app, and they never leave your phone:

  • A cache of analysis results (for example a small image fingerprint and a blur/brightness score) so repeat scans are fast
  • The photos you chose to keep while swiping, so they are not shown again
  • Your settings (trash option, haptic feedback, sort order) and your total space freed
  • Whether Roomy Pro is active on this device

You can clear all of this at any time from Android Settings > Apps > Roomy > Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads

The free version of Roomy shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and app interaction data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Learn more in Google's advertising policy. Roomy Pro removes ads. We never give Google or anyone else access to your photos.

Purchases

Roomy Pro (weekly, yearly or lifetime) is bought through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is active, so we can unlock Pro on your device.

Internet use

Roomy works offline for scanning and cleaning. It uses an internet connection only to check your purchase or subscription status with Google Play, and to load and show ads in the free version. No photos or media data are sent during these requests.

Data we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or the contents of your photos and videos. We do not sell your personal data.

Children's privacy

Roomy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
