Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

WebToApp lets you turn a website, or your own HTML page, into an Android app. You choose the content, name, icon, colours and features, and we build the app for you on our servers. This policy explains what information WebToApp collects and how it is used.

Information you give us

Google sign-in. You sign in with your Google account. We receive your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID from Google.

Your app details. To build your app we store what you enter: the website address or HTML you provide, the app name, package name, version, icon image, theme colour, and the features and navigation tabs you choose.

Built files. The APK and AAB files we build for you are stored so you can download them, until you delete the app or your account.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your apps and builds on any phone
  • To read the website address you provide and build your app
  • To let you download, update and rebuild your apps
  • To provide support when you contact us
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse

Information we do not collect

WebToApp does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, microphone or camera. The only file you can give us is the icon image you choose yourself using Android's own picker. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

Services we use

WebToApp relies on Google services to work:

  • Firebase Authentication and Google sign-in, to identify you
  • Cloud Firestore, to store your account profile and app details
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing, for sign-in support and optional purchases

These services have their own privacy policies, for example Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Purchases

Some features, such as downloading your built app files, may be offered through optional one-time paid plans. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is active.

Apps you create

The apps you build are yours. You are responsible for their content and for giving your own users a privacy policy that matches the features you turn on. Google Play requires a privacy policy page for every published app.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and app data for as long as your account is active. You can delete any app from inside WebToApp, which removes its settings and built files. You can also use Account > Delete account to permanently delete your profile, apps and builds. You may also email us to ask for your data to be deleted.

Security

We use Google's secure infrastructure and access rules so that your apps are visible only to you. No method of storing or sending data is 100% secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children's privacy

WebToApp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
