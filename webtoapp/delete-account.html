Request deletion from inside the app

You can delete your WebToApp account and all its data directly from the app, in a few taps:

  1. Open WebToApp and sign in with the same Google account
  2. Go to Account (bottom navigation)
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Confirm with your Google account when asked

Your account is deleted as soon as you confirm this step — no waiting period, no support ticket needed.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, email us at saifeestudio786@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my WebToApp account". We will verify your identity and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, photo and account ID
  • Every app you created in WebToApp — its website address or HTML, name, package name, icon, theme colour, features and navigation settings
  • Your built APK and AAB files
  • Your build history

This is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is kept

Google Play keeps a record of any purchase you made (for billing, tax and fraud-prevention reasons) even after your WebToApp account is deleted — this is handled entirely by Google Play, not by us, and follows Google's own retention rules. We do not keep a copy of your data once your account is deleted on our side.
