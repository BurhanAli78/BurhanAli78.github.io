STATUS SAVER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Status Saver lets you view and save WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business statuses, right from your own phone. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Information we collect

None. Status Saver collects no personal information, has no account or sign-in, and sends nothing to any server. The app does not even request internet access - open the app's permissions and you will not find it listed.

How the app works

You pick the WhatsApp/WhatsApp Business status folder once using Android's own folder picker, and the app remembers that permission so you don't have to repeat it. It only reads that one folder, never any other files or folders on your phone.

When you save a status, it is copied straight into your own Gallery (Pictures/StatusSaver or Movies/StatusSaver) using Android's MediaStore, the same system every gallery app uses. The app has no separate storage permission beyond this.

What stays on your phone

A few small settings are saved locally in the app, and never leave your phone:

  • The folder permission you granted
  • Your chosen theme (system / light / dark)
  • Whether Auto Save is turned on

None of this is personal information, and none of it is ever sent anywhere.

Third-party services

Status Saver uses no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs of any kind. There is nothing to share your data with, because nothing leaves the app.

Children's privacy

Status Saver is not directed at children under 13, and since the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy
